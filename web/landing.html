A theology map is what one person actually believes, written down and sorted by how much weight each belief carries — from the things they would build a life on to the things they hold loosely and are still working out.

It is not a quiz and there is no score. Nothing appears in your map that you did not put there, and nobody sees a draft until you are ready.

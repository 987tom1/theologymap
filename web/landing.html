How it works

Answer one question at a time, in your own words or by picking a position. Then mark two things on each belief:

  • Importance — T1 essential to the gospel, T2 church-defining, T3 important but not divisive, T4 a matter of liberty (from Gavin Ortlund's "theological triage").
  • Certainty — how sure you are, from certain to rejected.

No quiz, no score. Nothing appears in your map that you didn't put there.

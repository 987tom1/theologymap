Who believes what?

Every belief carries a tier — how much weight it holds, from T1, essential to the gospel, down to T4, matters of liberty, with T1.5 and T2.5 for the ones that genuinely sit between two ranks.

And a confidence — how settled it is: certain, confident, leaning, open, rejected. That is an ordinal band rather than a percentage, and it is independent of whether the belief is still being studied.

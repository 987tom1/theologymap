   POSITION ethics.prosperity-teaching/covenant-right  [Covenant right]
      hold: Material prosperity and physical health belong to believers as a covenant right, to be received through faith expressed in positive confession, so that poverty and sickness are, in the ordinary case, evidence of insufficient faith rather than God's will.
      vs:   Treating prosperity and healing as gifts God gives at his own discretion rather than as guarantees any believer's faith can secure.
      - held_by pentecostal / minority
        citation: Kenneth Hagin, The Believer's Authority (1984), representative of Word of Faith teaching
        note: Associated with independent Word of Faith teachers within the broader Pentecostal-charismatic movement; the Assemblies of God's own Statement of Fundamental Truths does not teach this, and its 1980 position paper explicitly critiques it.
      position sources: [{"label": "The Believer's Authority", "citation": "Kenneth Hagin, 1984"}]
   POSITION ethics.prosperity-teaching/prosperity-denied  [Not a guarantee]
      hold: Material prosperity and physical health are not guaranteed to believers by covenant right; faith is not a formula that secures wealth or healing, and teaching that treats it as one distorts the doctrine of faith and burdens the poor and unhealed with blame for their own suffering.
      vs:   Teaching that sufficient faith, rightly confessed, guarantees material prosperity or physical healing to any believer who exercises it.
      - held_by pentecostal / confessional
        citation: Assemblies of God, The Believer and Positive Confession (1980)
        note: The Assemblies of God's own official position, formally distinguishing biblical faith from positive-confession teaching.
      - held_by reformed / majority

      hold: The Great Commission includes Jewish people without any distinction from other peoples, so the church is to proclaim the gospel to Jewish people just as it does to everyone else, in fulfilment rather than betrayal of God's covenant with Israel.

   POSITION missions-and-world-religions.israel-and-judaism/no-institutional-mission  [No institutional mission]
      hold: The church neither conducts nor supports any institutional mission specifically directed at Jews, while continuing to bear witness to faith in Jesus Christ and never denying that salvation flows from Christ alone.
      vs:   Directing an institutional missionary programme specifically at Jewish people in the same way the church directs mission toward other, non-covenanted peoples.

   POSITION ethics.divorce-and-remarriage/oikonomia  [Oikonomia]
      hold: Marriage is ordinarily indissoluble, but the church, exercising pastoral economy for human weakness, may permit a second or third marriage through a penitential rite once a marriage has effectively died through adultery or desertion.
      vs:   Treating the church's pastoral economy toward a marriage's death either as a Catholic-style finding that no marriage existed or as an unqualified license to remarry without repentance.
      - held_by orthodox / confessional
        citation: Rite for a Second Marriage (Evchologion); canonical tradition following St Basil the Great
        note: The subsequent marriage is celebrated with a distinct, more penitential rite, marking it as an act of pastoral economy rather than an ordinary sacrament.

   POSITION ethics.divorce-and-remarriage/exception-clause  [Exception clause]
      hold: Divorce, and the remarriage of the innocent party, is permitted on the ground of a spouse's sexual immorality or desertion by an unbelieving spouse, though reconciliation is always to be preferred.
      vs:   Reading Scripture's exceptions as grounds for separation only, never for remarriage while the former spouse lives.
